# ♻️ CleanCred

> **Rewarding Responsibility | Turning Trash Disposal into Community Impact**

CleanCred is a gamified community platform designed to tackle public littering and infrequent cleanliness drives by directly incentivizing proper waste disposal.

---

## 🛑 The Problem

Public spaces suffer from unchecked littering, and traditional cleanliness drives are too infrequent to maintain long-term impact. Existing initiatives often lack continuous engagement mechanisms to motivate citizens to dispose of waste responsibly on a daily basis.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

**CleanCred** bridges the gap between civic responsibility and personal benefit. Users record themselves properly disposing of waste at verified dumping spots to earn **CleanCreds**, which can be redeemed for real-world rewards at local partner businesses and restaurants.

### 🛡️ Preventing Abuse & Fraud
To ensure authenticity and prevent cheating:
* **Live Camera Access Only:** Gallery uploads are strictly disabled—users must record live video/photos directly within the app.
* **Geofence Verification:** Submissions are validated using real-time GPS location checks to ensure the user is physically present at a pre-verified cleanup spot.

---

## ✨ Key Features (Current Release)

* 👤 **User Accounts:** Secure profile setup and credits wallet tracking.
* 📷 **Live Verification Engine:** In-app video/photo recording coupled with geofence validation.
* 📍 **Spot Requests:** Community-driven feature allowing users to suggest new dumpsters or litter spots for verification.
* 🎁 **Rewards Marketplace:** Display of partner restaurants and local businesses offering discounts for CleanCreds.
* 🤝 **Partner Portal:** Section for local businesses to register as reward partners.
* 💳 **Donations & Support:** Integrated funding tab to keep the platform and servers operational.
